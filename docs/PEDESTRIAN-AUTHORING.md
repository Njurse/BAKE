
## HIPS slot and toe binding (0.12)

- Every v5 entry gets slot 14 = HIPS at bake time: Tanner's HIPS mesh retargeted from page set 2 to the entry's legs page set (3rd texture page set). Paint the hips on your legs page at Tanner's hips rect (see textures/crops/HIPS_set2_t12.png in the Tanner source model); don't put other crops over that rect. HIPS stays a reference group in model.obj; editing its mesh is not baked yet.
- Feet are stored against the toe on their own side: `FOOT_LEFT` relative to `LTOE`, `FOOT_RIGHT` relative to `RTOE`, so each shoe sits on the joint that rotates it. The engine still draws slot 8 on `RTOE` and slot 11 on `LTOE` (the stock Tanner crossover), so the shoe art is mirrored by the draw — that is what a stock ped does and near-symmetric shoe art hides it. Storing a shoe against the opposite toe instead puts its art two toe-widths (56 engine units) from the pivot that turns it, which read as the shoes sitting shifted over and swinging wide. Author feet against the bindpose export (it uses this binding); the splitter refuses a part whose name and bone sit on opposite sides.
- Ship-with-MP characters live in JERICHO/MODS/mp/peds/<slug>/ (character folders as produced by import/add_character). BAKE_D1_CARS passes that folder to jmpped_player.py --characters; page sets are re-allocated on import, so character.json index/sets need not be unique across folders.
- tannerd3 is regenerated with: python tools/ped/jmpped_make_tannerd3.py --level <HAVANA.LEV> --out JERICHO/MODS/mp/peds (Tanner has no NECK slot in JMPPED; the head sits on the Jericho HEAD bone). Its upper-body parts keep Tanner's names (`TORSO`, `HEAD`, `U_ARM_*`, `L_ARM_*`), not the archive's `JERI_*`: the slots are addressed by number, so the exported models and materials read as Tanner.
